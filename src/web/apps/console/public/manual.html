N-Impression Logistics Operations Manual & Architecture Reference

A comprehensive, end-to-end operational and engineering reference for administrators, dispatchers, drivers, and technical auditors.

Jurisdiction & Regulatory Alignment
This platform operates under New Zealand transport, privacy, and employment legislation, specifically complying with the NZ Employment Relations Act 2000, Minimum Wage Order 2024 ($23.15/hr statutory floor), Wages Protection Act 1983, and the Privacy Act 2020.
Chapter 1

Platform Overview & Business Context

N-Impression Logistics Ltd is a specialized commercial road transport and urban freight delivery operator based in Auckland, New Zealand. The platform serves as the mission-critical digital backbone connecting fleet operations, real-time dispatching, driver field workflows, and automated payroll reconciliation.

Fleet & Compliance Management

Automates tracking of Vehicle COF/WOF renewals, Rego expirations, and Road User Charges (RUC) distance milestones to eliminate heavy vehicle downtime and non-compliance fines.

Dual-Basis Statutory Payroll

Calculates remuneration across both Hourly and Trip-rate bases, automatically adopting the higher gross and guaranteeing compliance with the statutory $23.15/hr minimum wage floor.

Real-time Invalidation Sync

Low-latency SignalR event distribution notifies dispatchers and drivers of live operational updates with zero stale-state drift through lightweight cache invalidation signals.

Chapter 3

Real-Time Dispatch Lifecycle & State Machine

Delivery tasks follow a deterministic state machine strictly audited at every transition. The architecture leverages SignalR to broadcast cache-invalidation pulses, prompting client instances to refresh without data payload overhead.

Dispatch State Machine & Invalidation Flow

Operational Step-by-Step Flow:

  1. Task Creation: Dispatcher specifies pickup and destination addresses, target time windows, and cargo priority.
  2. Resource Allocation: System validates driver operational area assignments and vehicle availability before binding.
  3. Driver Acknowledgment: Driver receives instant notification on mobile terminal and confirms readiness.
  4. Real-time Telemetry: Vehicle odometers and delivery timestamps are captured upon arrival and final drop-off.
  5. Immutable Audit Logging: Every state change writes an audit record and triggers asynchronous Outbox dispatch.
Chapter 4

Dual-Basis Payroll & Statutory Floor Engine

Under the NZ employment framework and company collective agreements, driver remuneration is governed by a Dual-Basis Engine implemented in PayrollCalculatorV2.cs. The system evaluates both an hourly timesheet basis and a per-trip piecerate basis, always remunerating the employee at whichever gross figure is higher.

Dual-Basis Calculation & Statutory Floor Decision Tree
Statutory Floor Guarantee: $23.15/hr
If a driver on Trip Basis experiences traffic delays resulting in an effective hourly wage below the NZ statutory minimum ($23.15/hr as codified in StatutoryMinimumWageRate), the system automatically injects a Minimum Wage Top-up line item to guarantee full legal compliance.
Chapter 5

NZ Wages Protection Act 1983 & Traffic Fines

A critical architectural feature of N-Impression Logistics is the strict separation between Traffic Infringement Notices and Employee Payroll Deductions.

Traffic Fine Handling & Wage Protection Flow
Legal Constraint: Zero Wage Deduction ($0.00)
Section 4 of the NZ Wages Protection Act 1983 strictly prohibits employers from making unilateral deductions from an employee's wages for traffic fines without explicit, uncoerced prior written consent. The platform renders a dedicated Fines Partition on payslips for informational transparency while enforcing exactly $0.00 deduction against net pay.
Chapter 6

Privacy Act 2020 DSR & Tamper-Proof Audit

Enterprise data governance is anchored on two cryptographic and database-level invariants: hardware-accelerated AES-256-GCM encryption for Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and an append-only PostgreSQL trigger on the audit ledger.

Cryptographic Storage & Immutable Audit Architecture

Data Subject Requests (DSR) & Right-to-be-Forgotten

In adherence to Principle 6 (Access) and Principle 7 (Correction) of the NZ Privacy Act 2020:

  • Data Export: Generates a machine-readable JSON archive containing all decrypted profile data, timesheet records, delivery history, and payslips.
  • Pseudonymisation / Deletion: Irreversibly replaces personal names, phone numbers, and IRD details with cryptographic hashes while preserving anonymized trip distance metrics for statutory transport reporting.
Chapter 7

Technical Architecture & Outbox Pattern

The backend application is engineered using Clean Architecture, CQRS with MediatR, and ASP.NET Core 9 running atop PostgreSQL.

1. Domain Layer

Pure enterprise entities, value objects, domain events, and business invariants with zero external package dependencies.

2. Application Layer (CQRS)

MediatR commands and queries, fluent validation pipelines, business handlers, and cross-cutting transaction decorators.

3. Infrastructure Layer

EF Core 9 PostgreSQL DbContext, Outbox worker dispatchers, AES-256 encryption providers, and SMTP email services.

4. Web API & SignalR Hub

RESTful controllers, JWT Bearer authentication, role-based authorization filters, and real-time WebSocket push hubs.

Guaranteed Message Delivery: The Outbox Pattern

To prevent distributed transaction failures between the relational database and external notification brokers, domain events are written atomically into the OutboxMessages table within the same database transaction. A dedicated background worker continuously polls and processes pending events with exponential backoff retries.
